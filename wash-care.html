Extend the Life of Every Garment

Proper fabric care directly impacts skin safety. Incorrect washing can break down hypoallergenic coatings, release microplastics, and cause irritation-triggering residue buildup.

Wash Instructions

🫧

Machine Wash 30°C

Use cold machine wash, gentle cycle. Best for organic cotton and Tencel.

🫧

Machine Wash 40°C

Standard warm wash. Safe for most conventional cotton garments.

🤲

Hand Wash Only

Delicate hand wash in cold water with pH-neutral soap. For silk, bamboo, and fine merino.

🚫

Do Not Wash

Dry clean only. Avoid water contact to prevent damage to structured garments.

Dry Instructions

🔄

Tumble Dry Low

Use dryer on low heat setting. Add a dryer ball to fluff natural fibers like linen.

☁️

Line Dry / Air Dry

Hang to dry naturally. Preserves bamboo and Tencel fiber integrity and reduces energy use.

🚫

Do Not Tumble Dry

Heat-sensitive fabrics like silk and superfine merino must always air-dry to prevent shrinkage.

📐

Dry Flat

Reshape while damp and dry horizontally. Prevents merino knitwear from stretching out.

Iron Instructions

🔥

Iron Low Heat (110°C)

Gentle iron for synthetic blends and delicate silk. Always use a pressing cloth.

🌡️

Iron Medium (150°C)

Cotton and linen — iron slightly damp for best crease removal.

💧

Steam Iron

Use steam to naturally relax fiber structure without direct contact pressure.

🚫

Do Not Iron

Polyester and acrylic melt under direct heat. Never iron printed graphics.

Bleach Instructions

🧪

Do Not Bleach

Chlorine bleach destroys natural dyes and weakens organic cotton fibers.

✅

Oxygen Bleach OK

Non-chlorine, oxygen-based whitening is safe for most undyed natural fabrics.

Professional Instructions

👔

Dry Clean Only

Professional cleaning required. Common for tailored suits, structured silk dresses.

💧

Wet Clean Allowed

Professional wet cleaning is a gentler, eco-friendly alternative to solvent dry cleaning.

💡 Pro Skin-Care Laundry Tips

  • •Use pH-neutral, fragrance-free detergent if you have eczema or dermatitis.
  • •Always double-rinse to remove all detergent residue — a major irritation cause.
  • •Wash new garments before first wear to remove manufacturing chemicals.
  • •Turn dark garments inside-out to preserve dye integrity and reduce microplastic shedding.
  • •Fabric softeners coat fibers with chemicals that block breathability — skip them for bamboo and linen.
