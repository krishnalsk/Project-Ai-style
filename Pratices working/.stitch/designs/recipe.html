Creamy Tuscan Garlic Chicken

schedule 30 min
restaurant 3 Servings
bar_chart Medium
star 4.7

Ingredients

Preparation

1

Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add chicken and cook until golden and cooked through, about 8 minutes per side.

2

Remove chicken from skillet and set aside. In the same skillet, add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

3

Stir in heavy cream, sun-dried tomatoes, and parmesan cheese. Bring to a simmer and let thicken slightly, about 3-5 minutes.

4

Add spinach and let it wilt. Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning the sauce over it, and serve immediately.
